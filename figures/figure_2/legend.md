**Figure 2. Counting units and spatial references affect duplication enrichment.** (*A*) Large-duplication enrichment within ±100 kb under five counting and randomization schemes for HBV, HPV and Epstein–Barr virus (EBV), shown on common axes. Points show observed/expected enrichment; whiskers span 95% spatial reference ranges, calculated by dividing observed counts by the 97.5th and 2.5th null percentiles. Indep. denotes independent placement and Cluster denotes cluster-preserving placement. Locus-based schemes use one representative coordinate per cluster under independent placement, and count distinct calls across the full cluster neighbourhood under cluster-preserving placement. The first four schemes match replication timing, gene density and fragile-site status. ᵃThe refined scheme adds covariates and amplification-peak exclusion, changing both matching and coordinate eligibility (Supplemental Material, Sections S1–S3). Lines connect categorical schemes; dashed segments mark the change in counting unit, and the horizontal reference marks enrichment of 1. (*B*) Rejection rates at nominal α = 0.05 for the first four schemes, under null catalogues generated by cluster translation or duplication-track rotation. Each cell descriptively pools four settings (HBV/HPV × ±100 kb/±1 Mb; 150 simulations each), which reuse simulation seeds. (*C*) Rejection rates for each setting, with 95% binomial Wilson confidence intervals. Each test uses 500 randomizations and an upper-tail add-one P value. Filled and open symbols denote ±100 kb and ±1 Mb; dashed horizontal lines mark the nominal 5% rate. (*D*) Mean coordinates per 100-kb leader cluster in catalogue-specific eligible sets. Coordinate/cluster counts are 3,645/2,642 for HBV, 4,086/2,550 for HPV, 115/99 for EBV, 237/103 for Sasa and 656/308 for Fan. The dotted line marks one coordinate per cluster; catalogue eligibility is detailed in Supplemental Material, Section S1.
