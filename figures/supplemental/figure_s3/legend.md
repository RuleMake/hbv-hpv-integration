**Supplemental Figure S3. Class enrichment and sensitivity in the supporting representation.** All panels use ±100-kb windows around the amplification-masked sets of 3,712 HPV, 3,138 HBV and 107 EBV coordinates. The interval-and-endpoint representation is defined in Supplemental Material, Section S1; the primary paired-call results are shown in Figure 4A and Table 1.

(*A*) All 24 virus-by-class-by-estimator estimates, with filled markers for record counts and open markers for cluster signs. Colours and shapes identify viruses in A and C; vertical bars show complete 95% spatial reference ranges. HPV and HBV share a vertical scale, with a wider scale for EBV. Stars mark the three estimates with Bonferroni-adjusted P < 0.05 across 24 comparisons, using two-sided probabilities approximated from upper-tail permutation probabilities. The reference contains 27,440 DEL, 32,553 DUP and 25,577 INV intervals, and 130,941 TRA endpoints.

(*B*) Natural-log ratios of HPV to HBV enrichment; positive values indicate greater enrichment for HPV. The class contrast sums the DEL and DUP log ratios and subtracts the TRA and INV log ratios. Grey rectangles extend from −MDE to +MDE, where MDE is the approximate minimum detectable effect at unadjusted two-sided α = 0.05 and 80% power. P values are unadjusted two-sided permutation values from 20,000 draws.

(*C*) Absolute deviation from a fold of one versus the approximate MDE, calculated by the normal approximation of 2.8 × null SD/null mean. Both axes are logarithmic; the line marks equality and the shaded region contains deviations below the MDE. Six of 24 estimates lie above the line; EBV depletions are included as absolute deviations. This comparison describes detection sensitivity alongside the statistical tests in A and B.

(*D*) Percentage of each record-count class excess contributed by the ten leading 100-kb integration clusters. Darker cells indicate larger shares, with exact percentages printed. Shares are defined for the five cells with a positive excess under the unadjusted one-sided test (P < 0.05); dashes mark the seven undefined cells. For HPV DEL, the share is 83.1% in this representation and 84.0% in the paired-call analysis; both analyses use overlapping SV records.
