**Supplemental Figure S5. Hotspot decomposition of structural-variant enrichment.** (*A*) Class-specific enrichment for all coordinates, recurrent hotspots and other sites, using a shared logarithmic scale. Estimates use the supporting interval-and-endpoint representation (Supplemental Material, Section S1), scoring overlap within ±100 kb of integration clusters. Recurrence is defined by distinct source-sample labels.

(*B*) Descriptive hotspot shares under source-sample recurrence and curated-gene definitions. Filled bars represent coordinates and open bars clusters; vertical ticks mark exact cluster-share endpoints, with subset counts and catalogue totals at right. The HBV gene-list hotspot subset contains three clusters. Coordinate counts partition each catalogue, whereas three clusters span both subsets under the gene-list definition, so cluster counts need not sum to the catalogue total.

(*C*) Enrichment against the separate large tandem-duplication catalogue under both hotspot definitions, with estimates for all integration coordinates as a reference. The viruses share a logarithmic scale, and estimates use the same window, counting rule and spatial null as A. The matched, amplification-masked sets contain 2,390 HPV and 2,421 HBV clusters formed at 100 kb.

In A and C, symbols mark observed-to-null ratios and shaded ranges show complete 95% spatial reference ranges. Each subset is compared with its own matched spatial null. The source data retain all 22 estimates and their unadjusted upper-tail empirical P values from 20,000 permutations, with a minimum attainable value of 1/20,001. Both hotspot definitions are applied after amplification-peak masking and describe different features from that mask.
