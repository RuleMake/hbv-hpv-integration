**Supplemental Figure S4. Window size and single-cluster influence.** All panels use the supporting interval-and-endpoint representation defined in Supplemental Material, Section S1.

(*A*) Record-count enrichment at ±100 kb (filled markers) and ±25 kb (open markers) for each virus and structural-variant class. Vertical bars show complete 95% spatial reference ranges; HPV and HBV share a vertical scale, with a wider scale for EBV. The analysis includes 3,712 HPV, 3,138 HBV and 107 EBV coordinates.

(*B*) Change in ±25-kb fold enrichment after removing one integration cluster at a time. Floating rectangles and end caps span the minimum–maximum changes across all 2,774 HPV or 2,673 HBV removals, relative to the full-set estimate. The horizontal line marks no change; the full-set fold and cluster count are printed below. The largest absolute change is 0.0216.

(*C*) The six largest-excess clusters for HPV DEL, HPV DUP and HBV TRA, ranked by observed minus expected counts. Zero-based bars and labels show their percentage contributions to class excess; coordinates are on GRCh37. HBV INV contribution shares are undefined under the unadjusted one-sided criterion (P = 0.212), and are identified in a separate text row. These ±25-kb six-cluster summaries complement the ±100-kb ten-cluster summaries in Supplemental Figure S3D.
