**Supplemental Figure S9. Boundary geometry and class composition across the HTLV-1 comparison groups.** ATL denotes adult T-cell leukaemia/lymphoma; single-study groups refer to PMID 35654946. Panel A extends the duplication-boundary analysis in Figure 3 across clinical groups; B–D use the supporting interval-and-endpoint representation (Supplemental Material, Section S1). Coordinate and cluster counts, plotted estimates and individual sensitivity scales are included in the source data.

Filled symbols denote absolute deviations at or above the approximate MDE; open symbols denote smaller deviations. The MDE uses the 2.8-null-standard-deviation approximation for two-sided α = 0.05 and 80% power. Symbols therefore classify effects against a power threshold; interval estimates are not displayed.

(*A*) Boundary-minus-interior displacement after excluding overlaps within 0, 1, 10 or 50 kb of a duplication endpoint. Displacement is the observed balance, (boundary − interior)/(boundary + interior), minus its matched-null mean, on a common scale across all eight groups. The numeric column gives MDEs at 0 kb; values for every radius are supplied with the source data. At 0 kb, HPV displacement is 0.0586 with an MDE of 0.0216; pooled HTLV-1 displacement is −0.00015 with an MDE of 0.00695.

(*B*) Composition displacements at ±1 Mb under covariate matching and additional matching on local breakpoint-density decile, showing all 16 group-by-null estimates. Each displacement is the observed composition score minus its null mean. (*C*) Thirteen pairwise differences between these displacements under each null. Squares denote pairwise contrasts, and the separator divides HBV and HPV comparisons. Composition-score displacements and contrasts in B and C are unscaled; multiplying by 100 gives the percentage-point scale used in Supplemental Figure S7.

(*D*) Class enrichment at eight window half-widths from 1 to 500 kb, showing 96 estimates on a logarithmic horizontal axis. Upper plots compare HBV and HPV, with a separate vertical scale for each class; lower plots show pooled HTLV-1 on a common vertical scale. Solid lines connect observed folds; dashed lines show the upper approximate MDE reference, coloured by virus in the upper plots and grey for HTLV-1. This reference is 1 + 2.8 × |(fold − 1)/z|, approximating 1 + 2.8 × null SD/null mean. Symbol fill compares the absolute deviation from one with the detectable deviation, including depletions; lines guide comparisons between tested windows.
