**Supplemental Figure S11. HPV integration clonality and nearby host structural variation.** (*A*) Subclonal fractions among 118 HPV junction records from 10 head-and-neck tumours, grouped by the presence of an SV within 100 kb in the same donor (Sasa et al. 2025). Labels give percentages and subclonal/total record counts. The pooled Fisher test gave P = 0.0023; a donor-stratified test that permuted proximity labels within donors across all 118 records gave P = 0.138 (20,000 permutations). Only four donors contributed records to both proximity groups. (*B*) Descriptive fractions for those four tumours, comprising 78 records already included in A. Both panels use published clonality labels linked to same-donor SV calls; the plotted units are junction records.
