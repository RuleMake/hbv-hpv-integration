**Supplemental Figure S6. Sensitivity of the external hepatocellular carcinoma analysis to case and region exclusions.** (*A*) Rearrangement-class enrichment within ±100 kb of HBV integration clusters, using the cohort's SV calls and a cluster-preserving spatial reference. All four settings exclude junction-proximal events within 10 kb. “None” applies no further exclusion; “Cases” removes all events from cases annotated with complex rearrangements; “Mask” excludes recurrent amplicon bins; “Both” combines these exclusions. Points show observed/expected ratios, and shaded rectangles show 95% spatial reference ranges on a common vertical scale. Masking reduces 1,340 coordinates in 839 clusters to 271 coordinates in 179 clusters; case exclusion reduces 14,414 events to 5,607. The tables report unadjusted one-sided permutation P values alongside approximate minimum detectable folds at two-sided α = 0.05 and 80% power.

(*B*) Ratios of mean per-case nearby-SV fractions in cases with detected integration versus HBV-positive cases without detected integration. Each case's fraction is the number of eligible SVs with an endpoint within 100 kb of an integration coordinate from another source sample, divided by all eligible SVs. Eligible events are intrachromosomal DEL, DUP and INV spanning 100 kb–7.5 Mb; a case contributes to a class mean only if it has an eligible event of that class. Group sizes are 296/99 for DEL, 256/89 for DUP, 247/79 for INV and 315/109 overall, in the order given above. Capped lines show 95% bootstrap confidence intervals; P values are from unadjusted one-sided Mann–Whitney tests.

(*C*) Scope of the case exclusion, with percentages and corresponding counts (n/N). Grey bars show the 6,075 annotated calls among 33,436 SV records and the 163 affected cases among 482 cases. Blue bars show the class-specific and total events removed from the analysis in A, amounting to 8,807 of 14,414 events. All events from an affected case are removed, while the integration-coordinate set is retained.
