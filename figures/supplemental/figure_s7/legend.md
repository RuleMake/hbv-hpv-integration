**Supplemental Figure S7. Genomic matching and the HBV–HPV composition contrast.** Seven matching schemes assess the composition contrast in a fixed set of 2,421 HBV and 2,390 HPV clusters at ±1 Mb. The composition score is (TRA + INV) − (DEL + DUP), using local class fractions in the supporting interval-and-endpoint representation (Supplemental Material, Section S1). All schemes preserve chromosome; replication timing, gene density and fragile-site status are matched separately, then jointly in 18 and 142 observed strata, followed by additional breakpoint-density matching. Panels A–C use 10,000 spatial permutations.

(*A*) HBV-minus-HPV contrasts between composition-score displacements. Points show the contrasts; shaded rectangles show normal-approximation 95% spatial reference ranges, calculated as the contrast ±1.96 null standard deviations. Effects and approximate minimum detectable effects (MDEs) are expressed in percentage points. MDEs correspond to two-sided α = 0.05 and 80% power; P values are two-sided and unadjusted.

(*B*) Virus-specific composition displacements under the same schemes, in the order used in A. Bars extend from zero; circles denote HBV and diamonds HPV. Short ticks mark approximate MDEs reconstructed as 2.8 times the null standard deviation from the rounded displacement and z score, on the corresponding positive or negative side. Filled symbols indicate absolute displacements at or above this power threshold; open symbols indicate smaller displacements.

(*C*) Percentage reduction in the contrast relative to chromosome-only matching. Solid bars show separately evaluated matching schemes. Hatched bars in the Arithmetic section summarize the sum of the three single-factor reductions and the additional reduction between the last two joint schemes. These arithmetic summaries do not partition the contrast into independent contributions.

(*D*) Contrasts and approximate MDEs for the two most detailed matching schemes, estimated with 10,000 or 20,000 permutations of the same observations. Both quantities are expressed in percentage points; full score values and the separately labelled arithmetic summaries are provided in the source data.
