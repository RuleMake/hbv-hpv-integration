**Supplemental Figure S1. Catalogue filtering and analysis units.** (*A*) Filtering of the integration records reported by Tian et al. (2022) to obtain the coordinate sets used in the analyses. The first two stages count records; the remaining stages count distinct coordinates within each viral catalogue on chromosomes 1–22 and X in the analysis grid. Mapping removes 248 records, deduplication and grid eligibility remove 479 more, matched-null eligibility removes one HPV coordinate, and amplification-peak masking removes 889 coordinates (374 HPV, 507 HBV and 8 EBV). Colours identify the viral catalogues; HPV and HBV counts are inside the bars, with EBV counts and totals at right. (*B*) The matched-null-eligible set supports the first four schemes in Figure 2A; the peak-masked set supports the class atlas and joint-corrected enrichment estimates. Mapped records in A belong to the same filtering sequence. (*C*) Donors, aliquots and structural-variant records in the reference data. The primary paired-call reference contains 152,235 calls from 1,972 aliquots and 1,902 donors; excluding donors with detected integration leaves 150,204 calls from 1,919 aliquots and 1,849 donors. The supporting interval-and-endpoint representation contains 216,511 records from 1,960 aliquots and 1,901 donors; its integration-excluded subset contains 213,671 records from 1,907 aliquots and 1,848 donors. This representation counts translocation endpoints and intervals for the other classes. The separate large-duplication catalogue contains 31,201 records from 1,972 aliquots and 1,902 donors, including eight chromosome-Y records excluded from analyses of chromosomes 1–22 and X. These records are grouped by donor and paired coordinates, whereas paired calls are counted at the aliquot level. Aliquot totals count individual identifiers, including those stored together in one metadata field. The overlapping reference sets are summarized separately.
