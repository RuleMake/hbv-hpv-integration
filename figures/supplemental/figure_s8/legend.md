**Supplemental Figure S8. HTLV-1 integration catalogue, class enrichment and sensitivity to study composition.** ATL denotes adult T-cell leukaemia/lymphoma; “single study” refers to PMID 35654946, which includes malignant and non-malignant samples. Panels C and D use the supporting interval-and-endpoint SV representation (Supplemental Material, Section S1).

(*A*) Assembly of the HTLV-1 catalogue from 51,806 VISDB records to 46,068 unique coordinates after cell-line exclusion, chromosome and position filtering, conversion to GRCh37 and exact-coordinate deduplication. (*B*) Contributions of seven studies to the leukaemia, non-malignant and unknown-clinical-state groups before spatial eligibility filtering. Stacked segments sum to 20,297, 14,361 and 11,410 coordinates, respectively. Studies are labelled by PMID, with separate segments for contributions to different clinical groups.

(*C*) Class enrichment within ±100 kb across eight groups and two estimators, giving 64 estimates from 20,000 spatial permutations. Within each pair, the upper point shows the record-count estimate and the lower point the cluster-sign estimate. Filled symbols indicate absolute deviations at or above the approximate MDE for two-sided α = 0.05 and 80% power; open symbols indicate smaller deviations. A cross denotes the one estimate with an undefined MDE. Stars mark the two-sided normal-approximation Bonferroni threshold across 48 HTLV-1 comparisons (|z| ≥ 3.279); HBV and HPV appear above the divider as reference catalogues. Coordinate and cluster counts and individual MDEs are supplied with the source data; pooled clusters are reconstructed and therefore need not equal the sum across component groups.

(*D*) Leave-one-study-out sensitivity of class enrichment. Capped spans show the minimum and maximum estimates, grey points show individual refits, and triangles show the all-studies estimates. The 16 spans summarize 64 refits with 5,000 permutations each: seven exclusions for pooled HTLV-1, four for ATL, two for non-malignant infection and three for unknown clinical state, across four classes. Only groups with multiple contributing studies enter this analysis; all individual refits are shown, with coincident values overlapping.

(*E*) Covariate balance, expressed as absolute standardized mean differences before matching (crosses) and after matching (open symbols). Dashed lines mark 0.10; replication timing uses a wider horizontal scale. The largest post-matching difference across the eight HBV, HPV and HTLV-1 groups is 0.0741. For EBV, the available post-matching mappability difference is 0.1264, above the balance guide; the other four covariates are unavailable and marked N/A.
