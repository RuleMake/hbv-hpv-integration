**Supplemental Figure S10. Tissue context and external integration catalogues.**

(*A*) Large-duplication enrichment within ±100 kb of HBV and HPV coordinates, scored in liver or cervical/head-and-neck tumour SV pools. Filled symbols show the corresponding tissue pool and open symbols the exchanged pool. The liver pool contains 3,279 duplications from 264 donors; the cervical/head-and-neck pool contains 366 duplications from 60 donors. Grey dots show 30 pan-cancer reference pools per comparison, generated by sampling the tissue pool's number of donors and capping the event count at that of the tissue pool. Realized donor and event counts vary; small vertical offsets separate the dots. Horizontal lines show 95% spatial reference ranges from 5,000 randomizations. The HBV estimate in cervical/head-and-neck tumours ranks third among the 31 tissue and reference estimates, giving an add-one reference-comparison P of 0.097.

(*B*) Composition displacements at ±1 Mb for Chinese Liver Cancer Atlas (CLCA) HBV, Sasa HPV, VISDB HBV and VISDB HPV, all scored against the same supporting PCAWG interval-and-endpoint SV reference. Coloured points and vertical lines show estimates and 95% spatial reference ranges; grey windows span −MDE to +MDE on a common vertical scale. Coordinate/cluster counts are 1,231/839, 217/93, 33,878/12,588 and 53,351/17,114, respectively. The Sasa set excludes PCAWG-derived records; VISDB filtering is described in Supplemental Material, Section S1.

(*C*) HBV-minus-HPV composition contrasts for CLCA–Sasa and VISDB under covariate matching (filled circles) or additional breakpoint-density matching (open squares). The main plot uses a common vertical scale; the inset expands the two VISDB estimates and their full reference ranges. P values are unadjusted, two-sided spatial randomization values from 20,000 draws for CLCA–Sasa and 10,000 for VISDB. The VISDB contrasts lie close to zero relative to their corresponding approximate MDEs of 0.00486 and 0.00414.

Reference ranges in B and C subtract reversed, centred null percentiles from the observed displacement or contrast and describe variation under spatial randomization of the fixed catalogues. Approximate MDEs describe sensitivity at 80% power and unadjusted two-sided α = 0.05. Composition-score displacements, contrasts, reference endpoints and MDEs are unscaled; multiplying by 100 gives the percentage-point scale used in Supplemental Figure S7. These comparisons vary the integration-coordinate source while retaining the PCAWG SV background.
