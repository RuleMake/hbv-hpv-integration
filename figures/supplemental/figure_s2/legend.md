**Supplemental Figure S2. Calibration of the block-translation null.** Three counting/null schemes are evaluated on 150 synthetic catalogues per virus and window size. Record-level counting scores each eligible coordinate separately. Locus-based schemes use leader clusters with a radius equal to the window half-width: independent placement retains one representative coordinate per cluster, whereas cluster-preserving placement counts distinct structural variants across each cluster's windows. Windows are ±100 kb or ±1 Mb; rearrangement-track rotation and the fourth counting/null combination are shown in Figure 2B,C.

(*A–C*) Rejection percentages at nominal α = 0.05, 0.10, 0.25 and 0.50 for record-level independent placement (A), locus-level independent placement (B) and locus-level cluster-preserving placement (C). The three schemes score the same synthetic catalogues, using 500 randomizations per test and a one-sided add-one probability; rejection is defined as P ≤ α. Upper and lower plots show ±100-kb and ±1-Mb windows on identical axes; dotted diagonals mark the expected rejection percentage under the null. Colours and shapes identify HBV and HPV; solid lines with filled markers denote ±100 kb, and dashed lines with open markers denote ±1 Mb.

(*D*) Rejection rates at α = 0.05 (left) and 0.01 (right), with 95% binomial Wilson confidence intervals across the 150 synthetic catalogues per setting. The main plots share a 0–46% scale; insets show the same locus-based estimates on a shared 0–10.5% scale. Within each scheme, positions from left to right are HBV at ±100 kb, HPV at ±100 kb, HBV at ±1 Mb and HPV at ±1 Mb, with the encodings used in A–C.

(*E*) Percentage of matched-null candidate positions outside windows around observed coordinates of the same virus, before amplification-peak masking. This separate diagnostic uses catalogues of 3,645 HBV, 4,086 HPV and 115 EBV coordinates, sampling up to 400 coordinates per virus and 20,000 positions per candidate pool. Percentages pool the sampled candidate counts. Points mark the three evaluated window half-widths on a logarithmic axis; connecting lines guide the eye, and EBV is labelled directly.

(*F*) Fold distributions across the 150 synthetic catalogues for the schemes in A and C. Boxes span the 5th–95th percentiles, whiskers the minimum–maximum range, and vertical marks the medians. The separate observed column gives the two available real-data estimates for record-level independent placement at ±100 kb; blank cells indicate unavailable summaries.
