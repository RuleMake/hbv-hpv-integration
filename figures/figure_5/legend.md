**Figure 5. Genomic and tissue context of the spatial association.** (*A*) HBV-minus-HPV composition contrasts under three replication-timing references: a consensus of 15 standardized ENCODE Repli-seq cell-line profiles, HeLa-S3 and HepG2. Each reference is applied to both viruses, retaining the same 3,054 coordinates in 2,359 HBV clusters and 3,626 coordinates in 2,340 HPV clusters across references. The score is (TRA + INV) − (DEL + DUP), using local breakend fractions; contrasts compare its observed-minus-null displacements between HBV and HPV. Values are multiplied by 100 for display in percentage points. Two-sided P values, Bonferroni-adjusted across the three references, are 0.0018, 0.0024 and 0.0006, respectively. (*B*) Non-hotspot HPV large-duplication enrichment under joint genomic matching, additional gene-overlap matching, and additional gene-length matching on a common set of 2,568 coordinates in 1,953 clusters. Identical complete breakend pairs are grouped within donors. Filled diamonds show call-count enrichment (labelled “Event incidence”); open diamonds show calibrated cluster-sign enrichment. Whiskers in A and B show 95% spatial reference ranges. (*C*) Mean large-duplication proportions for donors with and without detected integration within tissue groups. Each donor's proportion is the fraction of their large duplications with at least one breakend within ±100 kb of the shared, pooled coordinate catalogue for that virus. Donors with at least one catalogued large duplication are included. Bars show group means, and counts list detected and not-detected donors in that order. The first two rows weight donors equally. The squamous-only HPV row uses the cervical and head-and-neck squamous-carcinoma subset, with both groups standardized to common histology weights. Right, detected-to-not-detected ratios of means, with thick bands showing 95% confidence intervals from 50,000 donor-bootstrap replicates.
