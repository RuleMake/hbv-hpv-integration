**Figure 6. Transcript detection and local amplification at integration sites.** (*A*) Large-duplication enrichment at HPV integration sites with and without detected viral–human fusion transcripts (Fan et al. 2023). The groups contain 124 coordinates in 73 clusters from 60 patients and 532 coordinates in 274 clusters from 77 patients, respectively; some patients contribute to both groups. Diamonds show observed/expected enrichment, and whiskers show 95% spatial reference ranges; the between-group comparison is reported in Supplemental Material, Section S7. (*B*) Observed HBV integration clusters within amplicons from the same tumour, compared with matched spatial-null means (625 clusters from 899 coordinates in 193 samples). Circular amplicons follow the source study's classification (Chen et al. 2024); printed expected counts are rounded. (*C*) HBV junction read support and amplicon copy number, with one point per tumour sample. Inside amplicons, both axes show sample means of log₂ values over the contributing integrations (33 samples, 87 integrations). Outside amplicons, the response is mean log₂ junction read support (30 samples, 191 integrations). Its predictor is the log₂ of the median of the copy-number values assigned to amplicon-resident integrations in the same tumour. These 30 samples are a subset of the 33 inside-amplicon samples. Lines are separate, unweighted ordinary least-squares fits with intercepts, restricted to each panel's observed copy-number range. Slopes are 1.028 (95% t confidence interval, 0.350–1.705; two-sided P = 0.0042) inside and 0.029 (−0.420–0.478; P = 0.8963) outside, using 31 and 28 residual degrees of freedom. Marker area increases linearly with integration count above a fixed minimum, using the same scale in both panels.
